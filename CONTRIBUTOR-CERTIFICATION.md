# Stack contributor certification v1

When you submit a pull request to this repository, tick the checkbox on the first line of the PR template from your own GitHub account. You can do this in GitHub’s web editor. A Git sign-off, public email address or separate agreement is unnecessary.

> I certify under Stack Contributor Certification v1 that I may submit this contribution under the applicable repository licenses.

**What you certify.** You created the material you are submitting or have permission to submit it under the applicable terms in [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md). Original workflow descriptions and structured editorial data use CC BY 4.0. Validation scripts and workflow configuration use MIT. Identify any third-party material, its source, license, required attribution and applicable restrictions. A link to an external resource grants no rights in that resource. If the terms for a file or component are unclear, resolve them with a maintainer before merge.

**Who can certify.** Certify only what you have authority to submit. Identify coauthors, other rights holders and any employer, client or other permission required for the submission. Obtain the necessary permissions before submitting. Each additional rights holder whose permission is needed confirms their part in a PR comment from their own account or through a verified authorized representative. Existing third-party license permissions can be documented at their source. Maintainers verify any separate confirmations, authority and public credit, and arrange a private exchange for sensitive evidence. Keep private information out of the repository and PR.

**Scope.** Your certification covers material you intentionally submit in this PR, including your own later edits under this version. Identify changes in authorship, rights or third-party material and reconfirm the affected material. A materially changed certification requires fresh confirmation. GitHub keeps a public record of the PR, account and contribution. Submit only details you can make public.

You retain your rights, subject to the applicable licenses and permissions. This certification asks for no copyright assignment, separate patent grant or general right to relicense. Existing license terms and notices still apply. External tools, models and marks keep their own terms. Auny LLC may decide whether to publish or decline a submission. Posting an issue, review or discussion by itself gives no certification or permission to republish.

This project-specific certification governs new submissions once adopted. Earlier permissions remain governed by the terms on which they were given. See the [previous agreement](https://github.com/aunysillyme-dev/stack/blob/b48cbbc58199a54cf85bb8000ea2fe3f2f9b8438/CLA.md).
