# Video stack: freemium resources

Tools and platforms with a free tier alongside paid upgrades.

Reviewed 2026-11-28 · 18 entries · Source: https://aunysillyme.dev/stack/video/i2v-model-inventory.csv

- **[Kling AI](https://kling.ai/)**: Video generation | Platform | Freemium (`freemium`): Video and image studio for text, image, and reference prompts.
- **[Luma](https://lumalabs.ai/)**: Video generation | Platform | Freemium (`freemium`): Creative platform for video and image generation with Ray and Uni models. The site says to try it free.
- **[Pika](https://pika.art/)**: Video generation | Platform | Freemium (`freemium`): Creative platform for AI video, image, and audio, including hosted routes to other generators.
- **[Veo](https://deepmind.google/models/veo/)** (`veo-3.1`): Video generation | Model | Freemium (`freemium`): Google DeepMind page for Veo 3.1 video generation.
- **[Hailuo](https://hailuoai.video/)** (`MiniMax-H3`): Video generation | Platform | Freemium (`freemium`): MiniMax video and image studio. The homepage currently features MiniMax H3.
- **[Seedance](https://seed.bytedance.com/en/seedance)** (`Seedance-1.0`): Video generation | Model | Freemium (`freemium`): Official ByteDance Seedance page, including Seedance 1.0 video examples and benchmarks.
- **[Vidu](https://www.vidu.com/)**: Video generation | Platform | Freemium (`freemium`): AI video generator for text, image, and reference workflows.
- **[Viggle](https://viggle.ai/)**: Video generation | Platform | Freemium (`freemium`): Video and 3D workspace for remakes, scenes, characters, and motion.
- **[Descript](https://www.descript.com/)**: Editing | Editor | Freemium (`freemium`): Text-based AI editor for video and audio. The site says to try it free.
- **[Kapwing](https://www.kapwing.com/)**: Editing | Editor | Freemium (`freemium`): Browser editor that generates and edits videos from a prompt.
- **[Opus Clip](https://www.opus.pro/)**: Editing | Editor | Freemium (`freemium`): Turns long videos into short clips and can publish them to social platforms.
- **[VEED](https://www.veed.io/)**: Editing | Editor | Freemium (`freemium`): Video workspace for generation, editing, dubbing, and subtitles.
- **[Captions](https://captions.ai/)**: Editing | Editor | Freemium (`freemium`): AI video generator and editor aimed at ready-to-share social cuts.
- **[DaVinci Resolve](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve)**: Editing | Editor | Freemium (`freemium`): Blackmagic editor and color tool with a free version and a paid Studio upgrade.
- **[invideo](https://invideo.io/)**: Editing | Editor | Freemium (`freemium`): AI video editor for creating and revising clips in the browser.
- **[Magnific](https://www.magnific.com/)**: Upscaling | Upscaler | Freemium (`freemium`): Creative platform whose tools include video and image upscaling plus generation.
- **[Sync](https://sync.so/)**: Lip-sync | Lip-sync | Freemium (`freemium`): Lip-sync and visual dubbing studio and API.
- **[HeyGen](https://www.heygen.com/)**: Lip-sync | Lip-sync | Freemium (`freemium`): Avatar and talking-video platform. The site says to try it free.
